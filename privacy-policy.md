# MobileSEO Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MobileSEO handles information when you use the app or contact support. It applies to the MobileSEO Android app, package name `abiral.mobile.seo`.

For privacy questions or requests, contact [abiralacharya8@gmail.com](mailto:abiralacharya8@gmail.com).

## 1. How MobileSEO works

MobileSEO audits individual web pages and helps you organize findings, track fixes and create reports. No account or profile is required for core audits. Pages and related public site files are requested directly from your device, and the returned HTML and response data are analyzed on your device.

MobileSEO does not operate an account or audit server to which your workspace is automatically uploaded. The app does not include advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

## 2. Information processed in the app

- **Website URLs and page information:** URLs you enter or select are used to fetch pages and run checks. Results can contain page titles, descriptions, headings, links, image information, structured data, response information and other content extracted from the page. A URL or page can itself contain personal information.
- **Links shared from other apps:** On Android, MobileSEO can receive text or links that you choose to share with it. It extracts web URLs locally and prepares them for an audit. Receiving a share does not itself start a website request; you choose when to run the audit. Unused shared text is not added to audit history.
- **Local workspace:** The app saves up to 100 audit snapshots, including their URLs, dates, extracted page information and findings. It also saves project names and URLs, creation dates, favorites and in-progress fixes.
- **Preferences:** Appearance, accessibility options, website-platform selection and guided-audit preferences are saved locally.
- **Preview and report input:** You can edit preview text and enter optional project/client names, a brand name and notes for reports. Report text entered on the export screen is held for that screen and is not saved back to your project. Exported reports can contain that text.

These details support the features you use, including audits, saved history, comparisons, fix tracking, preferences and report generation. MobileSEO does not ask for your website or CMS login credentials, and its fix guides do not publish changes to your website.

## 3. Website requests and external content

Running an audit makes requests to the selected website and related resources, such as `robots.txt` and sitemap files. Optional link checks can also request linked pages. Requests may follow redirects to other hosts.

Project favicons and social-preview images may load from the website or another image host when the relevant screen or preview is displayed, including when you view saved results. These image requests can occur without starting a new audit.

The operators of those websites, image hosts and their infrastructure providers can receive ordinary request information, such as your IP address, the requested URL, request headers and the time of the request. They handle this information under their own policies. MobileSEO does not control their content, logging or retention practices.

The bundled sample audit and its guides can be explored without website, PageSpeed, favicon or social-image requests.

## 4. Google PageSpeed Insights

When you choose **Run PageSpeed**, MobileSEO opens Google's PageSpeed Insights website in your default browser and passes it the audited page URL for a mobile performance analysis. Google can receive that URL and ordinary request information, including your IP address, and request and analyze the page.

This optional service is handled by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Your browser may also process browsing data under its own settings and policies.

New PageSpeed reports are viewed in the browser and are not fetched or stored by MobileSEO. If reports from earlier in-app PageSpeed checks are present, you can remove those cached reports in **Settings & Help**.

## 5. Reports, sharing and clipboard actions

PDF and CSV reports are generated on your device. Depending on the selected options, they may include audited URLs, findings, dates, project/client names, a brand name, notes, fix status and comparison evidence.

When you choose to share or save a report, Android and the receiving app or service handle the report. A temporary file may be created in the app's cache to support sharing. Copies saved or shared outside MobileSEO are controlled by you and the receiving app or service; clearing MobileSEO history does not delete those copies.

When you choose a copy action, the selected text, code, link or support address is placed on the system clipboard. Clipboard handling is controlled by your operating system and any app into which you paste the content.

## 7. Storage, retention and your controls

The workspace and preferences use local app storage. Audit history is limited to 100 snapshots; older entries can be removed as new entries are saved. Projects and preferences remain until you change or remove them or the app's data is cleared.

You can manage information using these controls:

- **Delete an individual audit** in Projects to remove that saved snapshot.
- **Clear audit history** in Settings & Help to remove saved audits and their comparison evidence. This keeps saved projects, in-progress fixes and preferences.
- **Remove a project** in Projects to remove its saved project details and fix tracking. Its audit snapshots remain in history until separately deleted.
- **Clear the PageSpeed cache** in Settings & Help to remove reports saved by earlier in-app checks.
- **Clear app storage or uninstall** through Android settings to remove the app's local data. System-managed backups or restored data may be handled separately.
- **Delete exported copies** through the file manager, receiving app or service where you saved or shared them.

Your operating system, device manufacturer or backup provider may back up or restore app data according to your device settings. MobileSEO does not operate its own cloud backup or synchronization service. Review your device's backup controls to manage backed-up copies.

Workspace data held only on your device cannot be retrieved or deleted remotely by MobileSEO support. Use the local controls above to manage it.

## 8. Permissions and security

MobileSEO uses internet access for live website checks and external images. The Android app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location permissions, or broad access to shared device storage. Sharing uses Android's sharing interface and access to the selected report file.

Saved workspace information relies on the app's local storage and the access controls provided by your operating system; MobileSEO does not add a separate encryption layer to that storage. Website URLs without a scheme default to HTTPS. The protection of external requests also depends on the URL and destination service.
